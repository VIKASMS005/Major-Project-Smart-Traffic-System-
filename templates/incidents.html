How incidents are detected
Every vehicle is tracked between frames. A vehicle that stays in the same spot for longer than the configured time while other traffic in its direction keeps moving is reported as a possible accident, breakdown or blockage. Its signal group is then skipped in the cycle until the vehicle moves, so green time is not wasted on a blocked lane.
