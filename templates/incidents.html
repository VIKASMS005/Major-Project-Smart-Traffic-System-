How incidents are detected
Every vehicle is tracked between frames. A vehicle that stays in the same spot for longer than the configured time is reported as a possible accident, breakdown or blockage. Its approach is then skipped in the signal cycle until the vehicle moves, so green time is not wasted on a blocked lane.
