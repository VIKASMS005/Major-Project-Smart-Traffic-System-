Pipeline

  1. Data acquisition: traffic camera / CCTV video, or footage you upload.
  2. Preprocessing: Gaussian denoising and CLAHE contrast normalisation of each frame.
  3. Detection layer: YOLOv8 (a CNN-based single-pass detector) finds cars, motorcycles, buses and trucks. ByteTrack gives each vehicle a persistent ID.
  4. Traffic analysis layer: vehicles are counted per lane, and each lane's density is classed LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH.
  5. Incident detection: a tracked vehicle that stays still for longer than the threshold raises an alert (possible accident, breakdown or blockage).
  6. Signal optimisation layer:
    • Adaptive: green time = minimum green + seconds per vehicle × queue, clipped to 10–60 s.
    • Reinforcement learning: a Q-learning agent observes the queue density on both approaches and chooses the green duration that minimises total waiting time. It is trained over hundreds of simulated hours.
  7. Output layer: annotated video, live metrics, alerts, CSV logs and comparison charts.
