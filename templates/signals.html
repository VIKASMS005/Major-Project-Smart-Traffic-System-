How green time is decided

When an approach's turn comes, the controller reads the live vehicle count from its camera and sets

green = 10 s + 2 s × vehicles  (10–60 s)

followed by 3 s yellow and 1 s all-red clearance. Approaches with an active incident are skipped until the lane clears.
